# Models

Status: **confirmed** as a structure. Individual keywords, abilities and skills are
written up one by one when they are implemented.

## Profile

| Characteristic | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Movement | Inches the model may move with a move action. |
| Ranged | Dice modifier (+N or -N dice) on ranged attack rolls. |
| Melee | Dice modifier (+N or -N dice) on melee attack rolls. |
| Armour | Modifier to injury rolls made against the model. |

## Battlekit

Each item belongs to one category: ranged weapon, grenade, melee weapon, equipment,
armour, shield, special.

## Abilities, upgrades and skills

- Abilities come from the unit itself (for example a unit with an Aim ability),
  from optional upgrades, or from battlekit.
- Elite models may also have skills.
- Keywords on models and weapons modify the base rules case by case.
