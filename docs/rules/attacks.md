# Attacks and injuries

## Ranged attack

- Requires line of sight (`line-of-sight.md`) and the target within weapon range.
- Make a success roll. On a success, make an injury roll against the target.

Dice modifiers:

| Source | Modifier |
|---|---|
| Ranged characteristic | as on profile |
| Long range | -1 |
| Target in cover | -1 |
| Elevated position: attacker stands at least 3" higher than the ground the target stands on | +1 |
| Keywords, abilities, skills | as stated |

## Melee attack

- The attacker must be in melee with the target (within 1").
- Make a success roll. On a success, make an injury roll against the target.

Dice modifiers:

| Source | Modifier |
|---|---|
| Melee characteristic | as on profile |
| Defended obstacle: something at least 1/2" high lies between the two models | -1 |
| Keywords, abilities, skills | as stated |

## Injury roll

- Roll 2d6, with dice modifiers:

| Source | Modifier |
|---|---|
| Melee attack against a Down target | +1 |
| Blood markers spent by the attacker's player | +1 each |
| Weapon keywords, abilities, skills | as stated |
| Target's Armour | see `models.md` |

- Result (assumed bands, unconfirmed):

| Total | Result |
|---|---|
| 2–6 | One blood marker on the target. |
| 7–8 | One blood marker, and the target goes Down. |
| 9+ | The target is Out of Action. |

- A Down result on a model that is already Down places one extra blood marker on it.

## Bloodbath

- The attacker's player may spend 6 blood markers against a standing target, or 3
  against a Down target, to make the injury roll a 3d6 roll instead of 2d6.
- All other modifiers apply as usual, keeping three dice (see `rolls.md`).
